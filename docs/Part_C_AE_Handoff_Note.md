# Part C — Account Executive (AE) Pre-Meeting Handoff Note

**Target Client**: Vardhan Steelware (Jagadhri, Haryana)  
**Deal Context**: Re-engaging after 2023 ERP churn; high urgency for Diwali season delivery  
**Author**: Solutions Engineering  

---

## 1. Stakeholder Map & Decision Dynamics

* **The Economic Buyer (Decider)**: **Suresh Vardhan (58, Owner)**
  * *Signs the cheques, owns P&L.* Skeptical of software after losing ₹1.2 Lakh on a failed ERP in 2023. Non-tech; lives on WhatsApp and phone calls.
  * *Win condition*: Looking at his smartphone at 7:30 AM and knowing exactly where every delayed order is stuck without calling Ramesh 15 times a day.
* **The Champion / Influencer**: **Kunal Vardhan (30, Son, MBA)**
  * *Maintains the order sheet.* Craves modern tech, AI forecasting, and marketplace integrations to look professional before distributors.
  * *AE Strategy*: Validate Kunal's long-term vision, but steer him to solve the immediate shop-floor bottleneck first to prevent his father from killing the deal.
* **The Actual System Users (The Gatekeepers of Success)**:
  * **Ramesh (Dispatch In-Charge, 22 yrs with Suresh)**: Floor veteran. Carries all factory state in his head and pocket diary. If the software requires typing or complex menus, he will boycott it.
  * **Munshi Ji (Accounts)**: Guardian of Tally and credit limits. Needs clean reconciliation of outsourced polishing weight loss and payment hold flags.

## 3. What the Client Asks For vs. What They Actually Need

* **What Kunal Is Asking For**: Amazon/Flipkart API sync, predictive AI sales dashboards, and a flashy distributor portal.
* **What Suresh & The Business Actually Need**: A bulletproof, mobile-first **"Atka Maal Tracker"** that gives Suresh live order status, lets Ramesh confirm partial dispatches with 2 taps, and reconciles karigar metal shortages before payout.
* **AE Positioning**: *"Kunal, we will build your AI forecasting and Amazon sync in Phase 2. But if Ramesh doesn't update the floor status before Diwali, the AI will just forecast wrong data from a broken factory. Let's get Ramesh live in 7 days first."*

---

## 4. Anticipated Objections & Battle-Tested Scripts

* **Objection 1**: *"Pehle wale ERP mein ₹1.2 lakh doob gaya. Ramesh aur karigar kabhi software nahi chalayenge."*
  * **Counter**: *"Suresh ji, that ERP failed because it was built for an air-conditioned office desktop. Ramesh bhai had to sit and type in English. What we are giving Ramesh has zero typing—just big green/red buttons with utensil photos in Hindi. If Ramesh can use WhatsApp, he can use this in 5 minutes."*
* **Objection 2**: *"Diwali season sar pe hai. Abhi software lagane ka time nahi hai."*
  * **Counter**: *"Suresh ji, that is exactly why we are here today. If you wait until after Diwali, you will spend the next 4 weeks answering 30 angry distributor calls a day asking 'Mera maal kahan hai?'. We deploy the 3 morning reports on your phone in 7 days with zero disruption to your machines."*
* **Objection 3**: *"Munshi ji Tally chhod ke naye software pe nahi aayenge."*
  * **Counter**: *"Munshi ji doesn't touch anything new. He keeps using Tally exactly as he does today. Our system gives him a 1-click button to download verified dispatches straight into Tally."*
