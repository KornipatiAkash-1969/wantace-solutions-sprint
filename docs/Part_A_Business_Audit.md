## 1. Audit Notes & Findings

### 1.1 How Orders Arrive
* **65% WhatsApp**: Customers send photo screenshots of previous cartons, purchase orders (POs), or revised text (e.g., changed MRP, batch numbers, or barcode images) directly to the owner's personal WhatsApp.
* **25% Phone Calls**: Distributors and brand owners call Vikas to lock urgent repeat dispatches and negotiate delivery dates.
* **10% Walk-in Samples**: Local factory managers bring physical sample cartons to match paper burst-factor (BF), GSM, and flute specifications.

### 1.2 Step-by-Step Order-to-Delivery Lifecycle & Ownership
1. **Order Intake & Rate Confirmation (Owner - Vikas)**: Receives inquiry, manually checks raw kraft reel paper stock, confirms price per box, and forwards WhatsApp note to floor supervisor.
2. **Job Ticket (Parchi) Creation (Supervisor - Dinesh)**: Writes a physical pink job slip detailing ply (3-ply/5-ply), dimensions ($L \times W \times H$ in inches), sheet flute type, and quantity. Locates the client's stereo rubber block and cutting die in physical rack storage.
3. **Fluting & Corrugation (Machine Master - Ram Milan)**: Kraft paper reels (120–160 GSM) are loaded onto steam-heated corrugation rollers to produce corrugated rolls, pasted into rigid 3/5-ply sheets, and rotary-sheared to gross size.
4. **Flexographic Printing & Slotting (Flexo Master - Bablu)**: Polymer stereo plates are mounted on cylinders; water-based flexo ink is mixed; sheets are printed and slotted.
5. **Die-Punching & Wire Stitching (Stitching Team - 4 Workers)**: Sheets pass through a flatbed punch for interlocking lock-bottom folds; seams are wire-stapled or cold-glued.
6. **QC, Bundling & Staging (Supervisor - Dinesh)**: Boxes are counted into bundles of 25, strapped with polypropylene tape, and stacked at the bay.
7. **Gate Dispatch & Loading (Tempo Driver & Watchman)**: Loaded into hired Tata Ace / Eicher tempos. Dinesh signs a manual 3-copy delivery chalan.
8. **Invoicing & Payment Collection (Munshi Ji)**: Signed proof of delivery (POD) returns 1–2 days later; entered into Tally Prime for GST invoice generation. 30–45 days credit cycle.

### 1.3 Current Tracking Tools
* **Physical Job Ticket (Parchi)**: Paper slips clipped to clipboards hanging from machine control panels.
* **Supervisor Whiteboard**: A $4 \times 3\text{ ft}$ magnetic board in the floor cabin listing running job numbers and target tempo times.
* **WhatsApp Driver Group**: Ad-hoc group ("Apex Loading") where drivers send gate photos.
* **Tally Prime**: Run strictly on a desktop in the back office by Munshi ji for accounting; completely disconnected from factory floor operations.

### 1.4 The Single Most Costly Recurring Problem
* **Stereo Block / Artwork Version Mismatch During WhatsApp Change-Orders**: Clients frequently message revisions over WhatsApp (e.g., *"Update MRP to ₹179 and add new QR code"*). The owner forgets to forward the message, or the supervisor pulls the old stereo plate stored in the rack without verifying version history. The error is only detected at final QC bundling or when the client rejects the delivery tempo at their receiving bay.
* **Quantified Cost**: Occurs 1 to 2 times every month. Scraps 1,200 to 2,000 printed corrugated boards per incident, causing direct material loss of **₹18,000 to ₹32,000** per event, plus an emergency 48-hour re-run that halts scheduled orders and triggers penalty charges.
